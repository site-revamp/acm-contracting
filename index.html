Specialist capability

Water mist technology. A different way to protect.

Water mist systems use fine water droplets to absorb heat and help suppress fire. Their lower water usage compared with conventional sprinkler systems can be valuable in applications where consequential water damage is a consideration.

ACM’s water mist offering includes VID FIREKILL solutions. Suitability depends on the protected hazard, system design and applicable approvals.
