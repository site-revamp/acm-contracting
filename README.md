# ACM Contracting Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

ACM Contracting Pte Ltd provides fire protection engineering, installation, testing, commissioning, and maintenance in Singapore, with specialist expertise in water mist systems. The proposed homepage helps building owners, consultants, and project teams understand its capabilities and start an engineering enquiry.

## Main Improvements

1. **Consolidate the duplicated water mist announcement.** Present the VID FIREKILL announcement once in a dedicated section. This would free space near the top of the homepage for a clear introduction to ACM’s services and a direct enquiry action.
2. **Give engineering enquiries greater prominence.** Focus the main navigation and opening section on fire protection solutions, project requirements, and technical support. Give store functions a dedicated route so visitors can quickly find the appropriate next step for an engineering project.
3. **Explain credentials and show relevant experience.** Present the supplied BCA registration and industry affiliations with readable descriptions. Add approved project references that explain ACM’s role and the systems delivered, helping prospective clients assess its capabilities.

## Design Improvements

A focused opening section and consistent service summaries would make ACM’s engineering scope easier to scan. Dedicated sections for water mist technology, credentials, and project references would organise the supporting information, with enquiry actions beside relevant services.
